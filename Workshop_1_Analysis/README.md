# Workshop 1 – Systems Analysis

**Food Waste Reduction Platform**  
*Connecting cafeterias & restaurants with the community for surplus food redistribution*

## Overview
A geolocation-centered mobile application designed to reduce food waste in Bogotá, Colombia. The platform connects food donors (university cafeterias and restaurants) with recipients, giving **mandatory priority to verified charitable organizations**.

Recipients interact with an interactive map where surplus food donations appear as location pins, allowing them to discover, filter, and claim available food within a strict 3 km radius.

## Purpose
Reduce food waste while maximizing social impact through efficient redistribution of surplus food, prioritizing verified charities before general users.

## Key Objectives
- **General**: Develop a geolocation-based platform that facilitates real-time surplus food redistribution with priority for verified organizations.  
- **Specific**:
  - Define clear system boundaries (3 km radius, same-day operation).
  - Identify stakeholders, inputs, processes, and outputs (IPO model).
  - Document 17 measurable requirements and 10 critical risks.
  - Perform sensitivity analysis and complexity evaluation.
